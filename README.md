# Smart Orthopedic Bed: The Next Revolution in Sleep & Health 🛏️✨

## The Problem
Back pain and orthopedic issues affect the quality of life of millions worldwide. For many, the type of mattress and sleeping posture is the difference between a night of suffering and proper recovery. However, current beds are static furniture, unable to adapt to real-time medical needs. 

## The Vision
We are proposing a dynamic, AI-driven smart bed that changes its shape and characteristics based on personal medical data:
- **Dynamic Pillow Adjustment:** Automatically adjusts height for optimal cervical support.
- **Adaptive Firmness:** Changes the sleeping surface from hard to soft based on precise orthopedic needs.
- **Cybersecurity First:** As a medical device, the bed must be 100% immune to cyber attacks. We are prioritizing rigorous security protocols to prevent malicious actors from altering the bed's configuration and endangering the user.
